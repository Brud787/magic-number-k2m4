their sources. It accompanies certificate publication; it is not a new
mathematical research campaign or an independent human review. Current source
links and publication metadata were checked during the release preparation
on 2026-09-28. Exact arithmetic checks and symbolic arguments remain distinct
from external citation checks. The bibliography keys below are stable even
when the manuscript's section numbering changes.
