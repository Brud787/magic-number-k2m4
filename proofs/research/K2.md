# Exact rank-two laboratory

Audit date: 2026-09-05. Scope: first-run infrastructure, not a classification of amplituhedron tiles or tilings.

## Classical source-cell normal form

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** A loopless rank-two positroid is determined by parallel classes that are cyclic intervals. See Mohammadi–Zaffalon, *Combinatorics of essential sets for positroids*, Section 6, Proposition 6.1 and Corollary 6.2 ([2024 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.09453v3#S6)); that paper credits their earlier *Computing positroid cells in the Grassmannian of lines, their boundaries and their intersections*, Proposition 3.9 ([2022 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14001)). This is existing source-cell classification, not a new result about tiles. Their Theorem 5.1 gives the affine inversion codimension formula used independently below.

**PROVED (elementary reconstruction).** Allow loops, let $q$ be the number of nonloops, and let $s\ge2$ be the number of parallel classes after deleting loops. These classes partition the cyclically ordered nonloop support into intervals. The bases are exactly pairs from different classes. Conversely every such support and cyclic partition has a nonnegative realization: start the cyclic list at a class boundary $a$, choose strictly increasing rational slopes $t_1<\cdots<t_s$, and positive scales $x_i$. Give column $i$ in class $j$ the vector

\[
C_i=\epsilon_i x_i(1,t_j)^T,\qquad
\epsilon_i=\begin{cases}+1&i\ge a,\\-1&i<a.\end{cases}
\]

Loop columns are zero. Ordered two-by-two minors are positive between classes and zero within them: the sign on the wrapped columns exactly cancels the reversal of slope order across the cut. This also handles a class such as ((9,1,2)); treating every column scale as positive without the cyclic sign would be incorrect.

**PROVED (dimension calculation).** Before quotienting by row operations, a dense coordinate chart has $q$ scale parameters and (s) direction parameters. The free action of $GL_2$ on full-rank matrices has dimension four, hence

\[
\dim S=q+s-4.
\]

For dimension eight, $q+s=12$, so the only possibilities are

| Nonloops $q$ | Classes $s$ | Labelled loopless patterns |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 6 | 1 |
| 7 | 5 | 21 |
| 8 | 4 | 70 |
| 9 | 3 | 84 |
| 10 | 2 | 45 |

The third column counts choices of $s$ boundary cuts among $q$ labelled cyclic positions. Its sum is $221$ loopless source patterns across the five support sizes. Cyclic rotations are retained; this is not a count modulo symmetry. Inserting loop columns embeds these source patterns into arbitrary ambient $n$.

**PROVED (scope restriction).** Every eight-dimensional rank-two source cell has at most ten nonloops, for arbitrary ambient $n$. Thus arbitrary $k=2,m=4$ tiles, if their source cells satisfy the modern eight-dimensional convention, necessarily occur among embeddings of these finitely many loopless source types. This alone neither certifies any type's injectivity nor controls compatibility or global tilings. It is a consequence of the classical classification, not a novelty claim. Deleting loop rows of $Z$ preserves positivity; reverse extension or degeneration claims are not assumed.

**PROVED (source boundary rule).** The codimension-one positroid boundaries are obtained by deleting one element of a nonsingleton class or merging two neighboring classes, provided rank remains two. Indeed, basis containment can only remove support elements or merge surviving classes. A dimension drop of one forces ($q-q'$+$s-s'$=1), giving exactly these possibilities. Positivity forces the merger to be cyclically adjacent. Both operations are realized by limits in the above chart (rotating the signed chart for a merger across its cut). These are facets of source-cell closures. Their images may collapse or fail to be facets of tile images.

## Encodings and independent exhaustive checks

**COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED.** The program represents each source cell by cyclic parallel classes and loops, its complete basis set, a Grassmann necklace, and a bounded affine permutation. It enumerates independently in three ways:

1. Choose $q$ nonloops and (s) boundary cuts in their cyclic order. This gives $\binom nq\binom qs$ cells of type ((q,s)).
2. Enumerate every ordinary permutation, every valid fixed-point decoration, and the resulting bounded affine windows of rank two. Compute dimension as (2(n-2)-\ell(f)). Construct the Grassmann necklace and full basis set by cyclic Gale-order inequalities.
3. Enumerate every Le diagram in a (2\times(n-2)) rectangle with eight plus signs and enforce the Le avoidance condition. KWZ Lemma 2.5 identifies plus count with dimension.

The first two methods agree on the **entire set of affine labels**, not only its cardinality, and independently computed necklaces and basis sets agree. Le diagrams independently agree in cardinality. A pipe-conversion implementation additionally agrees with all affine cells in every dimension for $n\le7$. Source facets from the elementary generator agree with exhaustive dimension-seven basis-containment checks for every dimension-eight cell at $n=6,7,8,9$.

## Exact map samples and the injectivity obstruction

**COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED.** Both samples use (Z_{ij}=i^j), $1\le i\le n$, $0\le j\le5$, which has positive maximal minors by the Vandermonde product. Sample 1 uses scales (x_r=r), slopes (t_j=j-1); sample 2 uses scales (x_r=r^2+1), slopes (t_j=2^{j-1}). Every source realization is checked against its complete Plücker support using `Fraction` arithmetic.

For the differential, retain the redundant $q+s$ scale/slope variables, calculate their image derivatives (d(CZ)), append the four infinitesimal row-operation directions (A(CZ)), compute exact matrix rank, and subtract four. A rank-eight witness proves generic rank eight for this fixed $Z$, because a corresponding rational minor is not identically zero on the source chart. It establishes neither global injectivity nor the same conclusion for every positive $Z$. A lower-rank sample establishes no generic upper bound.

**SOURCE-VERIFIED / COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED.** Galashin–Lam, *Parity duality for the amplituhedron*, Example 13.12, identifies a four-mass-box cell of algebraic degree two and explicitly says it is not admissible ([paper](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~galashin/papers/duality.pdf)). Their centered window (h=[2,1,4,3,6,5,8,7]) becomes our bounded window (h+2=[4,3,6,5,8,7,10,9]), encoding ((8,1)(2,3)(4,5)(6,7)). A cyclic rotation is ((1,2)(3,4)(5,6)(7,8)). For the latter, the first sample has rank seven and the second rank eight. Thus this one regression demonstrates both accidental sample degeneracy and the failure of generic full rank to certify a tile. The published nonadmissibility statement is used explicitly; algebraic degree two alone is not silently equated with failure of injectivity on a positive real domain.

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Galashin–Lam Lemma 3.2 supplies a necessary bound on eight-dimensional source cells that could map to full-dimensional regions. In our bounded-window convention it is $f$i$\le i+n-4$ for every $i$. It leaves respectively $1,21,202,1203$ cells for $n=6,7,8,9$. Passing the bound does not prove full dimension, injectivity, or tilehood.

**COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED (independent differential check).** For the spurion, four-mass box, and one collapsed source cell, SymPy separately builds the symbolic matrix, normalizes the image in a Grassmannian coordinate chart, and automatically differentiates its eight coordinates. At sample 2 this gives ranks $8,8,5$, agreeing with the tangent-quotient code. This checks the gauge subtraction and analytic derivative rather than repeating the same implementation.

## What must come next, after the first-run stop

**CONJECTURAL / UNRESOLVED.** The next mathematical bottleneck is a criterion for all-$Z$ global injectivity of the bounded source types, followed separately by all-$Z$ compatibility and coverage. The current enumerator cannot feed a rigorous exact-cover formulation until those geometric predicates are certified. Full rank, pairwise sampled disjointness, or a canonical-form identity would each be inadequate substitutes. No complete $\mathcal A_{8,2,4}$ tiling classification, infinite (k=2) theorem, or new structural tile theorem is claimed.

## Run 2: finite all-Z source admissibility and parent relations

**PROVED.** If an eight-dimensional cell has a parallel class B with at
most four nonloops outside B, its image lies in the locus of planes
meeting `span(Z_i:i outside B)`. Choosing a line in that span and a plane
through it bounds image dimension by `outside_count+3<8`. Invariance of
domain excludes injectivity for every positive Z. This proves a structural
non-tile filter without Jacobian sampling.

**SOURCE-VERIFIED / COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED.** Explicit general BCFW
product/zero/dihedral recipes certify176 source labels at n8 and939 at n9,

## Run 5 — finite tiling generation and a complete inverse at n8

**PROVED.** D7=L7 and D8=L8. Exactly seven n7 all-Z tilings have six
tiles each. Every n8 all-Z tiling has twenty tiles, and every such
collection is exactly a binary solution of the certified103×176
indicator system. Its binary solutions have not been exhaustively counted.

Every n8 admissible source has a class D with exactly five complementary
nonloop labels. The hyperplane through those five external rows recovers
one source row uniquely. A positive six-row compressed system recovers
all remaining slopes and D coefficients. The five strict inequalities,
all cyclic signs, and closure degenerations give a complete rational
inverse and open-neighborhood membership certificate. Independent source-
minor checks verify all18,128 saved entries. This yields the full
all-Z indicator kernel by an exact rank argument, not by generic-rank
classification. See [the proof](run5/N8_INDICATOR_THEOREM.md).

**CONJECTURAL.** Extension to n≥9. The spurion's (3,3,3) tile profile
has no class with exactly five complementary labels; the present inverse
does not cover it. Run4 already certifies its local parent relation,
but that is insufficient for the global kernel. No new arbitrary-n
tiling reduction is asserted.
