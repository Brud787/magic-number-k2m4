# Counterexamples sought and what the bounded search establishes

**PROVED.** No complete all-Z tiling difference lies outside the full
parent span at n7 or n8: D7=L7 and D8=L8. This conclusion uses complete
finite proofs, not absence of a counterexample in a sample. In particular
there are no unequal-cardinality all-Z tilings in these two cases.
The all-n generation assertion remains **CONJECTURAL**.

## The restricted-move failure was retained

**FAILED, preserved.** The old seven-support move set at n8 did not
generate even the differences of the standard-dihedral orbit. Run5
does not rediscover or weaken that obstruction. It uses the full Run4
span: 56 seven-support rows of rank 48, plus 40 eight-support rows of
rank 40, with combined rank 73. All fifteen independent differences of
the sixteen known standard-dihedral tilings lie in that full span, with
saved integer decompositions. Thus the previously missing larger-support
generators materially change the generation test.

The subsequent complete indicator certificate excludes an outside
difference for every all-Z n8 tiling. A bounded flip search separately
reaches a 73-dimensional difference span, establishing equality rather
than only inclusion. None of its 2,440 discovered states is interpreted
as an exhaustive list of complete tilings.

## Exact failures of proposed proof mechanisms

**FAILED.** Ordinary source boundary as the next differential after the
geometric parent map. Every one of the 96 n8 parent columns has nonzero
ordinary source boundary, even modulo two. The complete source boundary
does square to zero; deleting nonadmissible source facets causes the
failure. Source and image relations are different objects.

**FAILED.** The minimal coordinate quotient by nonadmissible source
closures detects exactly the geometric kernel. It gives a mod-two complex
with middle homology dimension fifteen and eight explicit single-tile
cycles. For example, the tile with singleton support {2,3,4,6,7,8} has
zero quotient boundary and coefficient sum one, but its image has
nonempty interior. This is a formal source cycle, **not an abstract
indicator relation**, local tile decomposition, or Magic Number
counterexample. The model fails before any such geometric inference.

**FAILED.** Distinct source-positroid tilings always have a common
positroid refinement under source-Bruhat refinement. An admissible
eight-cell has no proper full-dimensional source subdivision, so tilings
are minimal there. General semialgebraic intersections do provide finite
refinements, but their atoms are not thereby positroid tiles and their
relations are not thereby generated by the Run4 vectors.

**FAILED, preserved from prior runs.** Deleting four-mass terms, replacing
positive multiplicity by complex degree, assuming universal parent
coverage, or counting two fiber endpoints instead of wall types. Run5
does not remove the two hostile parents or the printed spurion
discrepancy from the research state.

## Direct functional check

**PROVED.** The exact n8 neighborhood matrix has a rational dual weight
vector with A^T lambda=1 and sum(lambda)=20. This assigns one to every
tile in the complete U8. Thirty coefficients are negative and some are
half-integral. It is a linear functional on the finite span of tile
indicator classes, using constant values on certified open neighborhoods;
it is not a positive volume or raw point measure on the quotient by
lower-dimensional sets.

This independent cardinality certificate agrees with generation at n8.
No analogous uniform all-n functional was established or assumed.
No degree-weighted or canonical-form identity was used to infer a new
geometric tile relation.
