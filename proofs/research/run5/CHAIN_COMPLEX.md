# Chain-complex results and obstructions

**PROVED.** At n7, the parity label dictionary identifies the 21 tiles
with the five-subsets of [7]. The ordinary abstract simplex complex
Q^7→Q^21→Q^35 has ranks six and fifteen and zero middle homology. All
seven parent rows match the oriented six-subset boundaries up to overall
sign. This is a finite combinatorial regression, not a claim that parity
identifies arbitrary indicator relations or the literal rank-two source
CW differential. The separate complete tiling proof establishes D7=L7.

**FAILED.** At n8 the ordinary source differential following the image
relation map fails even modulo two. Its 504×176 matrix has rank 176,
and every one of the 96 geometric parent columns has nonzero next
boundary, with 1,680 nonzero entries altogether. The full source chain
complex on all 266 eight-cells and 112 parents does square to zero;
dropping the inadmissible eight-facets breaks that identity. A nonzero
mod-two product cannot be fixed by changing integer orientation signs.

**PROVED / COMPUTATIONALLY-VERIFIED.** Quotienting the lower group by
the 408 seven-cells below any of the 90 nonadmissible eight-cells restores
a formal F2 complex

\[
 \mathbb F_2^{96}\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2^{176}
 \longrightarrow\mathbb F_2^{96}.
\]

Its two ranks are 73 and 88, leaving middle homology dimension 15.
This is a mod-two source calculation, not a rational image-homology rank.
More decisively, eight individual nonempty admissible tile vectors have
zero quotient boundary. The tile with singleton support {2,3,4,6,7,8},
affine (1,4,6,7,5,8,10,11), is one such cycle. All its lower facets
have explicit nonadmissible-closure witnesses. Its augmentation is one,
so it cannot lie in the span of the balanced local vectors, and its
image indicator is nonzero on an open set.

The unit-cycle obstruction also rules out an analogous coordinate
quotient as an exact rational complex with image L8: deleting all the
boundary coordinates of that tile makes it a cycle regardless of signs,
whereas its real augmentation remains one. This does not assert that
the displayed mod-two homology dimension is its rational homology
dimension. It identifies a structural mismatch between source cycles
and geometric indicator relations.

All source incidences, products, independent field-rank algorithms and
explicit closure witnesses are in [SOURCE_CHAIN_TEST](SOURCE_CHAIN_TEST.md)
and [source_chain_test.json](../../results/run5/source_chain_test.json).

## The finite presentation that does work

**PROVED.** The independently certified n8 indicator matrix A gives an
exact rational sequence at its middle and right terms:

\[
 \mathbb Q^{96}\xrightarrow{R^{\mathsf T}}
 \mathbb Q^{176}\xrightarrow{A}\mathbb Q^{103}\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \rank R=73,\quad\rank A=103.
\]

There are 23 linear dependencies among the 96 parent generators. The
middle kernel is exactly L8. This is an algebraic presentation using
103 certified open indicator evaluations, not a cellular differential
to seven-dimensional target facets. Relabeling those evaluations as
“C7” would not create a topological proof. Its exactness follows from
the geometric inversion and determinant certificate, and is the reason
generation is settled at this finite n.

The characteristic is material: the saved indicator matrix has rank
102 in characteristic two, whereas its rank is 103 over Q. Its selected
integer minor has determinant two. No rational exactness claim is based
solely on a mod-two test.

## What a genuine all-n chain proof still requires

**SOURCE-VERIFIED / PROVED elementary orientation identity.** Proper
pushforward of oriented source boundaries naturally counts signed real
preimage degrees; it does not simply discard noninjective facets.
The hypotheses and derivation are recorded in
[CHAIN_COMPLEX_LITERATURE](CHAIN_COMPLEX_LITERATURE.md). Applying that
identity to amplituhedron parents requires full source/image charts,
compactification, orientations and all real branches.

**CONJECTURAL.** A suitable relative complex or mapping cone has the
required kernel generated by the Run4 admissible parent relations.
The collapsed-image strata do form a downward-closed source set, but
removing additional nonadmissible cells is a different quotient: their
positive image functions need not vanish. The four-mass parent and the
eight spurious unit cycles must be explained in any such model. The
present finite tests reject two shortcuts and do not establish all-n
homological exactness.
