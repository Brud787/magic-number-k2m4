# The exact generation problem and the finite bridge

## Spaces and conventions

Let U_n be the full set of eight-dimensional rank-two positroid source
labels whose amplituhedron maps are injective for every positive n×6
matrix Z. Define V_n=R^{U_n}, with its source labels as a free basis.
Coefficients and tile collections are fixed independently of Z. For a
complete all-Z tiling T let chi_T be its binary incidence vector. The
tiling convention is pairwise disjoint open images and closed coverage
with multiplicity one, without an extra common-face axiom.

Let C_Z be the vector space of semialgebraic constructible functions on
Gr(2,6), modulo functions supported in dimension at most seven. Define

\[
 \Phi_n:V_n\longrightarrow\prod_{Z\in\operatorname{Mat}^{>0}_{n,6}}C_Z,
 \qquad e_S\longmapsto(\mathbf1_{T_S(Z)})_Z,
 \qquad K_n=\ker\Phi_n.
\]

The product explicitly implements the all-Z quantifier; it is not an
evaluation at a generic or chosen external matrix. The coefficients are
constant real numbers. The finite matrices below have rational entries,
so their rational ranks equal their real ranks.

Let L_n be the span of **all** Run4 parent relations, including every
increasing zero insertion and dihedral image whose support fits in [n].
Let D_n be the span of all differences chi_T−chi_T' of complete all-Z
tilings. The sigma covector assigns one to each source tile label.

**PROVED from the definitions and the Run4 input.**

\[
 L_n\subseteq K_n,\qquad D_n\subseteq K_n,\qquad \sigma(L_n)=0.
\]

Neither L_n⊆D_n nor D_n⊆L_n follows from these inclusions alone. A local
relation need not be extendable inside a complete tiling. An arbitrary
global relation need not be generated by one-parent relations. In
particular, defining sigma on V_n/L_n does not make it constant on the
classes of complete tilings; that missing statement is still substantive.

The desired all-n bridge is D_n⊆L_n. The stronger K_n=L_n suffices, but
is not required by Magic Number. Flip connectivity is stronger in a
different way: it produces actual paths through complete tilings, while
linear combinations can use relations with no admissible application
at an intermediate vector.

## What the bounded run proves

**PROVED.** D7=L7, of dimension six, and there are exactly seven all-Z
tilings. Their genuine single-parent flip graph is a seven-cycle. This
sector is a source-derived cyclic-polytope/parity regression with a new
complete exact fixture; no novelty is claimed. See [N7_PROOF](N7_PROOF.md).

**PROVED.** K8=L8 and D8=L8, of dimension 73. The complete 176-tile
universe admits 103 exact open indicator constraints with a determinant-2
maximal minor. Thus K8⊆ker A=L8; the reverse inclusion is the Run4 input.
A separate bounded family of 74 genuine all-Z tilings gives L8⊆D8.
Consequently every all-Z tiling at n8 has twenty tiles. No complete n8
tiling enumeration or full flip-connectivity statement is needed.
See [the indicator theorem](N8_INDICATOR_THEOREM.md),
[the flip-span proof](N8_FLIP_SPAN.md), and their independent audit.

**PROVED.** The n8 all-Z tilings are exactly the binary solutions of
A*chi=1. This is a complete finite exact-cover formulation. Its necessity
comes from actual open indicator neighborhoods. Sufficiency uses the
already proved full kernel equality, positivity of binary coefficients,
and closed coverage; it is not inferred from a sampled exact cover.
We have not exhausted its solutions because the finite generation
problem has already been solved.

**CONJECTURAL.** D_n⊆L_n for every n≥9. The present work does not supply
an induction, an exact source-chain model, a Baues connectivity proof,
or a cardinality functional uniform in n. The requested all-n Magic
Number theorem remains open in this campaign.

## Why the local support bound does not finish generation

**PROVED limitation.** Run3/4 identifies each local parent with a core
on at most eleven labels. It does not bound the union of source supports
in a general vector of D_n or K_n. A relation can involve tiles spread
over arbitrarily many labels. Zero insertion transports a relation on a
subamplituhedron, but does not turn a complete smaller tiling into a
complete tiling of the larger amplituhedron.

Thus checking a finite list of local cores is not an all-n reduction for
D_n/L_n. Even an order-preserving system of maps V_r→V_n and L_r→L_n
does not supply the required maps on complete-tiling differences. An
inductive proof needs a new deletion/specialization operation on whole
tilings, or a kernel-generation theorem that avoids tilings entirely.

## Three ranked all-n architectures and kill-tests

1. **CONJECTURAL: a complete presentation of the indicator kernel.**
   Extend the n8 mechanism by proving that all coefficient dependencies
   among the exact tile membership functions are generated by the finite
   parent relations. The finite success uses independent lower and upper
   rank bounds, rather than connectivity. A general proof needs a
   symbolic rank bound or a controlled deletion filtration with kernels
   generated by known local rows. The first new test should include the
   genuine n9 spurion tile; the six-row complement-five inverse alone
   does not cover its (3,3,3) profile. A certified relation outside L_n
   kills this stronger kernel theorem, even if its coefficient sum is zero.
   A rank deficit in a chosen witness matrix alone does not kill it.

2. **CONJECTURAL: an additive unit functional sufficient only for tilings.**
   The exact n8 signed neighborhood functional gives each of its 176
   tiles weight one. Seek a uniform construction on all required tile
   classes, including spurions, or a weaker functional constant on
   complete-tiling classes in V_n/L_n. The half-integral weights and
   thirty negative entries in the current certificate provide no
   positive-volume interpretation or automatic recurrence. Kill-tests:
   a genuine tile with a different weight; loss of additivity modulo
   lower-dimensional sets; a relation with nonzero coefficient sum for
   a proposed universal functional. The latter kills that functional,
   not Magic Number unless realized by complete tilings.

3. **CONJECTURAL: a corrected relative pushforward or mapping-cone model.**
   Retain signed real-preimage degrees and the nonadmissible strata
   needed for chain compatibility, then prove that complete tiling
   differences bound chains whose admissible part is generated by
   Run4 relations. The direct ordinary source differential and the
   coordinate quotient by nonadmissible closures are already killed by
   [exact finite tests](CHAIN_COMPLEX.md). Any replacement must explain
   why their spurious single-tile cycles disappear and why positive-real
   four-mass branches are retained. Kill-tests: a nonzero boundary-square,
   a cycle with nonzero image indicator, or an unjustified identification
   of complex degree with positive-real multiplicity.

The unqualified common-positroid-refinement route is **FAILED** under
source-Bruhat refinement: a tile has no proper full-dimensional source
subdivision. A common semialgebraic refinement is a different object,
and does not provide the parent generators. A connected Baues-like
poset or connected plabic chart graph also needs a separate theorem
relating its moves to these tilings. [BAUES](BAUES.md) and
[LITERATURE_DELTA](LITERATURE_DELTA.md) record the precise source scope.

The run stops here before a long all-n search. No extrapolation from
n7,n8 ranks is used as an infinite generation theorem.
