# Independent review: the complement-five indicator circuit

**PROVED, independently reviewed.** The complement-five theorem can be strengthened from existence of one balanced relation to a complete description of the constant-coefficient indicator-relation kernel on its six admissible boundary tiles. For **every fixed positive \(Z\)**, that kernel is one-dimensional and generated by the primitive alternating \(3+3\) sign vector. In particular, every such relation has coefficient sum zero.

The finite universe in this statement is exactly the six selected source boundary tiles from SMALLER_CORES.md, Sections 1–4. It does not include arbitrary additional tiles whose images might lie in the parent image. It implies no global generation statement or Magic Number theorem.

## All nine cross-sign overlaps are realizable

Fix positive \(Z\) and arbitrary positive first-five source coefficients \(a_i\). The ordered compressed matrix \(B\), its alternating Gale vector \(w\), and all six inward derivatives are then fixed. Write the six strict source inequalities as

\[
u_h>0,\qquad
u=(t_2-t_1,\ldots,t_\ell-t_{\ell-1},d_1,\ldots,d_r),
\]
\[
L=(w_2-w_1,\ldots,w_\ell-w_{\ell-1},
     w_{\ell+1},\ldots,w_{\ell+r}).
\]

Here \(\ell+r=7\). The reviewed positive-minor argument gives three positive and three negative entries of \(L\), none zero.

Every positive six-tuple \(u\) is realizable by a source point: set the initial slope \(t_1=0\), recover later slopes by successive summation, and set the \(d_j\) to the remaining entries. This constructs one initial source point and its image \(Y\). It does **not** impose \(t_1=0\) along the fixed-image fiber. The complete fiber remains the affine Gale line, and its inequality values are

\[
u_h(\tau)=u_h+\tau L_h.
\]

For any chosen \(i,j\) with \(L_i>0\) and \(L_j<0\), assign

\[
u_i=L_i,\qquad u_j=-L_j,\qquad
u_h=2|L_h|\quad(h\ne i,j).
\]

All initial inequalities are positive, so this is a genuine parent source point. Its full positive fiber is exactly \(-1<\tau<1\). At \(\tau=-1\), only wall \(i\) is active. At \(\tau=1\), only wall \(j\) is active. The remaining values at both endpoints are at least \(|L_h|>0\); the other chosen inequality is also strictly positive. Thus both endpoint source points are in their respective **open** selected walls. The previously proved positive hyperplane functional keeps the source rank two at the endpoints.

Both endpoints map to the same \(Y\). Each selected wall map is globally injective and its open source cell has dimension eight. Invariance of domain therefore makes each open wall image open in the target. Their intersection contains \(Y\) and is a nonempty open set.

This constructs all nine cross-sign overlaps for each fixed positive \(Z\). Same-sign open wall images are disjoint by the complete interval theorem. Hence the graph of nonempty open overlaps among the six wall images is exactly \(K_{3,3}\).

## The exact indicator kernel

Let \(T_h\) denote the closed tile image and suppose

\[
\sum_{h=1}^{6}c_h[T_h]=0
\]

in the indicator quotient modulo semialgebraic sets of dimension below eight. For any cross-sign pair \(i,j\), take the nonempty open intersection just constructed. Same-side disjointness excludes all other open wall images there. The boundaries of the finitely many closed tiles have dimension at most seven, and the relation's exceptional set has dimension below eight. Their union cannot contain this open intersection.

At a remaining point, exactly \(T_i,T_j\) occur, and the relation forces

\[
c_i+c_j=0.
\]

All nine edges are realizable, so these equations imply that the three positive-sign coefficients have one common value \(\alpha\), and the three negative-sign coefficients have value \(-\alpha\). Conversely the reviewed \(3\leftrightarrow3\) theorem supplies precisely this relation. Therefore

\[
\ker\!\left(\mathbb R^6\longrightarrow
  \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{[T_1],\ldots,[T_6]\}\right)
 =\mathbb R\cdot\bigl(\operatorname{sgn}L_h\bigr)_{h=1}^{6}.
\]

Consequences within this six-tile universe:

- The indicator span has dimension five for every fixed positive \(Z\).
- The displayed relation is primitive over the integers and has no proper nonempty dependent support.
- Every constant-coefficient relation is balanced, including relations with coefficients beyond \(\pm1\).
- The all-\(Z\) relation kernel is the same line, because the stronger fixed-\(Z\) statement already holds for each positive \(Z\).

## Review outcome and limits

The argument uses free initial inequality values, the already certified entire affine fiber, and open wall images. It does not infer open overlaps merely from a point in two closed images. It does not silently constrain an additional slope gauge along the fiber. It does not infer a relation from sampled chamber values. No unbalanced relation or missing branch was found.

The statement is stronger than a finite list of overlap samples: its construction works symbolically for arbitrary positive \(Z\). For other parent profiles, exact overlap witnesses at only one \(Z\) can bound the kernel of **all-\(Z\)** relations but do not automatically establish a one-dimensional kernel at every fixed \(Z\). Preserve that distinction when combining catalogs.

No assertion is made about arbitrary tiles contained in the parent region, complete tiling extension, generation by parent relations, or priority. The classical circuit and fiber methodology and the precise relation to Galashin--Lam's stronger local/global conjectures remain as recorded in the targeted Run 3/4 literature notes.
