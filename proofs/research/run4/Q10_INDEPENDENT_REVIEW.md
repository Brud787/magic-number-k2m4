# Independent hostile review: the q10 (3,3,4) and (2,4,4) proofs

Reviewer: the hostile-core branch, independently of the author of `Q10_334.md`.

**Review conclusion: PASS for the stated local all-Z relations.** I found no substantive gap in the two ten-wall 5↔5 proofs, conditional on the already verified n9 wall admissibility and positive loop-extension certificates explicitly invoked by the proof. This is not an audit of relation generation, complete tilings, or Magic Number; none of those conclusions follows from these two relations.

The following checks address the main possible failure points.

1. **Source chart and all walls.** Three ordered directions have a unique projective normalization. Ten positive weights modulo common scaling give the full nine-dimensional parent. The ten deletion facets plus three mergers exhaust its codimension-one source boundaries. Each merger's complementary support has size at most four, so the all-Z Schubert obstruction applies. For source order (4,3,3), the four A-deletions are precisely the (3,3,3) spurions; the proof does not silently treat them as BCFW.

2. **Global rank.** In the dual dependence, annihilation of `Y` together with the three block conditions does imply `alpha(V)=alpha(W)=0`: first `alpha(U)=0` forces `alpha(V)=0`, then the second row forces `alpha(W)=0`. The determinant of `(Z_A,V,W)` is a strictly positive sum. It forces `alpha=0`, after which `beta` annihilates the six distinct B and D external rows. Thus the rank-eight claim is uniform in positive external Z.

3. **Fixed pivot.** Removing the selected two B-coordinates makes the eight-by-eight matrix block triangular. The determinant is the product of the two displayed quotient four-minors; changing quotient basis multiplies it by a positive square. The bracket `B1234` is positive. For (4,3,3), the other bracket expands in the positive b6,b7 weights with one inversion of Z5, so it is negative and remains strictly negative when either one of those two weights vanishes. For (4,2,4), the second bracket is a positive sum. Every selected open deletion wall retains nonzero U,V,W.

4. **Submersion and interval.** The differential kernel of the source-to-Grassmannian map at a positive point is `ker(M_Y)/R x`, so it has dimension one. This supplies the stated submersion justification directly, without assuming that a set-theoretic fiber dimension implies derivative rank. The pivot-sum normalization bounds both pivot coordinates between zero and one. Every other coefficient depends linearly on them, making the positive fiber a single bounded interval.

5. **Endpoint completeness.** A rank-two coefficient limit with at least two zero weights lies in a projective face of dimension at most seven. A rank-one limit in the normalized chart must retain an active diagonal B-column and must lose every A- and D-column. Its nonzero image line is supported in the external B-span. The incidence dimension bound is `(b−1)+4=b+3≤6`, so these limits cannot account for generic endpoints. Source mergers were separately excluded by their all-Z image-dimension bound. This is the needed argument that a compact coefficient interval does not hide a full-dimensional nongauge boundary.

6. **Orientation and actual symmetry.** For the (4,3,3) reflection, direct substitution gives the displayed `H C(x) J` identity. The six-row external sign exponent is `15−r(6−r)+6−r`; it is odd for every possible r, so multiplying by one fixed ambient determinant-negative G makes Z positive. For (4,2,4), ordinary reversal contributes the six-row sign `(-1)^15`. Both positive-coordinate permutations consist of five transpositions and reverse the source projective orientation. The action of any ambient G preserves the orientation of Gr(2,6), since its character is `sign(det G)^2`. The exact tangent orientation sequence therefore reverses fiber orientation. Global wall transversality and connectedness make the chosen wall signs constant, so the reflected wall pairs have opposite signs. The four spurion walls are paired within A; they are included in this argument.

7. **Geometric relation.** Every open coordinate wall has a nonzero inward kernel direction, so its image enters the positive parent. Generic fibers have exactly two admissible endpoints, one of each sign. Source-wall injectivity gives multiplicity one. Invariance of domain makes open tile images open, so a same-side overlap would meet the generic locus and contradict uniqueness. Compact closed tile images, source incidence, and generic endpoint coverage then yield equality of the closed covered regions. This proves the ordinary tile relation, not merely an oriented source identity.

The reflection argument is particular to these profiles. It should not be exported to parents without such an actual positive symmetry, and the rank-one endpoint bound should not be exported when the active pivot class has larger support. These limitations are already stated in the proof.
