# Run 3: targeted prior-art delta

Checked 2026-09-05. This supplements the preserved Run 1/2 literature audit. It is confined to the two-class Cramer argument, oriented fiber signs and the reflection criterion. No novelty claim or negative-search novelty certificate is made.

## Galashin--Lam already formulate the parent-flip problem

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Section 13.4 of *Parity duality for the amplituhedron* defines nearly admissible codimension-one parents using full-dimensional image and degree-one/collapsed boundary conditions. Conjecture 13.10 asserts two equal-size boundary triangulations and also says these are the only two triangulations of the parent image. Conjecture 13.11 separately asserts connectivity of complete triangulations. Example 13.12 gives the four-mass obstruction: one degree-two boundary together with eight degree-one boundaries can enter a canonical-form identity, without producing an admissible tile triangulation. The ensuing Baues-poset questions concern all subdivisions, not just a list of balanced local identities. [GL, PDF pp. 48–50](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~galashin/papers/duality.pdf).

**Assessment.** The Run 3 two-class theorem independently proves existence and balancedness of a restricted parent replacement through positive fibers. It does not prove the uniqueness clause of Conjecture 13.10 or the connectivity assertion of Conjecture 13.11. The n8/n9 quadratic-wall regressions instantiate the published degree/multiplicity warning; that obstruction is prior art.

## Cramer alternation is classical circuit structure

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Alternating signs of maximal-minor kernel coordinates are classical for cyclic configurations. Rambau's *Triangulations of cyclic polytopes and higher Bruhat orders*, Theorem 3.3 in the checked preprint, describes alternating circuits; Remark 3.5 relates upper/lower projected faces to nonoverlapping triangulations. [Author's paper, PDF p. 9](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~deloera/MISC/LA-BIBLIO/trunk/Rambau/Rambau1.pdf).

**Assessment.** The Run 3 formula for the `(m+1,m+2)` parent is a direct positive-minor/Cramer mechanism of this kind. The added work needed for its amplituhedron application is recovery of the unique positive first-class ray, exclusion of the negative second-row branch, all-Z wall injectivity and exact local image coverage. This targeted check does not establish whether the exact two-class statement was previously written down. It supplies no basis for calling the sign mechanism new.

## Fiber geometry, secondary objects and their hypotheses

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Mohammadi--Monin--Parisi, *Triangulations and Canonical Forms of Amplituhedra: a fiber-based approach beyond polytopes* (2021), already develops amplituhedron fiber parametrizations, fiber forms, Gale/secondary-fan constructions and a broader fiber-positive-geometry framework. Sections 4–5 include explicit linear-fiber models, intersection-vector determinants and Cramer calculations. The main secondary-fan statements in Sections 5.2–5.3 retain Conjecture 5.6's positivity and the stated combinatorial-independence hypothesis; they cannot be imported as unconditional assertions about arbitrary m4 parents. Section 7 explicitly distinguishes the broader proposed framework from the developed special cases. [Paper, especially pp. 11–24 and 30–32](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07254).

**Assessment.** Studying fibers or linking fiber boundaries to decompositions is established methodology. The Run 3 bounded contribution is verification of its needed geometric hypotheses for specified source profiles, together with exact failures for others. No general fiber framework, secondary amplituhedron or arbitrary canonical-form additivity theorem is claimed.

## Source signs and fiber signs remain different

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Olson's *Orientations of BCFW charts on the Grassmannian* gives algorithms for relative source-chart orientations and a signed source boundary operator; Section 4.2 proves that the operator squares to zero, and Section 4.3 applies its signs to residue cancellation. [Paper, pp. 23–25](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.6363); [published primary record](https://repo.scoap3.org/records/11636/).

**Assessment.** Those source-orientation results do not independently establish which image wall is entered or exited by an oriented positive fiber, admissibility, or union equality. Run 3's complementary-minor formula makes that image-side step explicit in the linear chart; it is itself standard Hodge-dual determinant algebra.

**SOURCE-VERIFIED.** Orientations of a short exact sequence determine the third orientation from the other two. This standard determinant-line rule is stated, for example, in Dan Freed's 2017 differential-geometry notes, Exercise 2.9. Applied to the tangent sequence of a submersion, it determines the fiber-orientation effect of a symmetry. [Author's notes, p. 4](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~dafr/M392C-2017/Notes/lecture2.pdf).

**Assessment.** The reflection criterion is an application of this rule. Its mathematical content in the n8 prototype lies in checking an actual symmetry, submersion, transverse walls, globally constant signs and the source/target orientation actions. A symmetric-looking facet list alone is insufficient.
