# Run 3 independent mathematical review

This records the bounded pass's proof audit. The author of a calculation
was not its sole reviewer. The root independently read each prototype
proof and its code; separate agents checked the two-class and core
transport theorems. Tests check exact formulas through different ambient,
quotient, combinatorial, or symbolic implementations. A passing test is
never used by itself to assert an all-Z theorem.

## What passed and why

checked as a normalized kernel wedge identity, including the sign

differences of positive numbers. The pivot is nonzero at the boundary

the connected open wall. These justify the symbolic sign continuation.
The argument uses the repaired fixture only, retaining the literal-source
discrepancy and the absence of a certified embedding into the printed
fifty-row tiling.

**PROVED: the seven-support sign mechanism.** The corank-one external
kernel has alternating nonzero minors. In a common positively oriented
image gauge every fiber point is C+h w^T. Its parallel-pair equation forces
h parallel to that pair, with a strictly nonzero coefficient. The selected
minor derivatives have alternating signs because consecutive w entries
have opposite signs. The common positive twistor bracket prevents an
orientation-reversing source representative from entering the argument.
The seven resulting vectors agree with the independently constructed
Run2 image relations, up to global reversal. Two collapsed facets remain
explicit. The source-count or known BCFW cardinality is not the sign proof.

**PROVED: new n8 interval relation and reflection count.** The three-anchor
normalization gives the entire positive source cell. Both rank and the
global pivot have positive-minor proofs throughout the parent and all ten
open selected walls. Every wall has an independent all-Z BCFW certificate;
wall injectivity rules out a positive within-wall fiber. The two-zero and
rank-one exceptional sets have image dimension at most seven and six,
respectively, so generic endpoints are complete. Openness and compact

An ambient twelve-equation elimination reproduces the quotient signs.
Reflection is an actual source/external symmetry: it permutes coordinates
by five transpositions, while the induced GL(6) action preserves the

without relying on a sign table.

**PROVED: the complete two-class sector and core transport.** Two independent
reviews passed; see [CORE_THEOREM_REVIEW](CORE_THEOREM_REVIEW.md).
